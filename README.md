# 📊 ConnectaTel — User Segmentation & Behavioral Analysis

**Customer Analytics Project | Python · EDA · User Segmentation · Data Visualization**

Analysis of user behavior and engagement patterns to identify key customer segments, potential churn risks, and opportunities to improve retention and business growth.

---

## 🛠️ Skills & Technologies

`Python` · `Pandas` · `Matplotlib` · `Seaborn` · `Jupyter Notebook` · `EDA` · `User Segmentation` · `Data Visualization`

---

## 🎯 Objective

Analyze user demographics and platform usage to identify behavioral patterns and opportunities for improving engagement and retention by evaluating:

- 👥 User distribution by age group
- 📊 Platform usage levels
- 🔁 Engagement and retention patterns
- ⚠️ Potential churn risks
- 📈 Growth opportunities across customer segments

---

## 👨‍💻 My Role

Developed the complete analytical workflow from **data exploration to customer insights**:

- Cleaned and explored user data using **Python & Pandas**
- Performed **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to identify patterns and anomalies
- Segmented users by **age group and usage level**
- Analyzed user distributions and engagement behavior
- Created visualizations using **Matplotlib and Seaborn**
- Identified customer segments with potential retention and growth opportunities
- Converted behavioral patterns into clear, business-oriented insights

---

## 🚀 Results & Impact

The analysis identified:

- The **adult segment represents the largest share of the user base**
- Most customers show a **medium level of platform usage**
- Only a small proportion of users reach **high engagement levels**
- Low-usage customers represent a potential **retention and churn risk**
- Younger users are **underrepresented**, suggesting an opportunity to expand the customer base

These findings highlight an opportunity to increase overall engagement by moving medium-usage customers toward higher activity levels while strengthening retention strategies for lower-engagement users.
